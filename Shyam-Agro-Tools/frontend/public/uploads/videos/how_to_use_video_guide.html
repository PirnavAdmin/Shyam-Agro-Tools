Overview
Biofit Antiviral Eco-Friendly Solution
Protect plant foliage and crops against viral diseases with 100% natural, eco-friendly broad-spectrum antiviral spray.
Step 1 of 3
Measure Correct Dilution Ratio
Measure 2 ml to 3 ml of Biofit Antiviral concentrate liquid per 1 Liter of clean water using the measuring cup.
Step 2 of 3
Mix Thoroughly in Sprayer Tank
Pour the measured Biofit liquid into your garden or agricultural knapsack sprayer and mix well with water.
Step 3 of 3
Foliar Spray Application
Spray evenly on upper and lower surfaces of crop leaves during early morning or evening for maximum antiviral protection.
