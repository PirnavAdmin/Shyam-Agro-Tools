Product Overview

Biofit K Potassium Mobilizing Bacteria

Organic liquid biofertilizer that mobilizes soil potassium for stronger roots, pest resistance, and high crop yield.

Step 1 of 3

Measure Recommended Dosage

Measure 1 ml to 2 ml of Biofit K concentrate liquid per 1 Liter of clean water using a measuring cup.

Step 2 of 3

Soil Application & Drenching

Mix well into sprayer tank or drip system and drench the soil around crop roots during early planting and growth stages.
